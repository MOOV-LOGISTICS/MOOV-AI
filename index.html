A / What it is

MOOV AI: the company AI portal

Access through Teams and the web portal

MOOV AI is available through Microsoft Teams and the web portal. The portal offers SSO alongside account login.

The intended access route for employees is the existing company identity.

Answers based on internal documents

Agents use MOOV documents to answer questions on company policies and procedures.

The Finance Assistant can be demonstrated with a question on MOOV reimbursement rules and a check of the cited source.

Permissions and usage visibility

IT manages access, the model catalogue and platform logs. These records support usage reviews and troubleshooting.

Usage reviews can help identify recurring questions and gaps in the available knowledge.

Consumer AI use

Personal AI accounts are outside the scope of our current platform usage records.

With MOOV AI

Company-managed access, internal knowledge sources and platform logs, with external model processing.

Dify and Kymo: responsibilities

Dify alone

A developer toolbox

  • SSO availability depends on the Dify edition and deployment
  • Permission settings need to be mapped to MOOV roles
  • Knowledge-base access requires explicit permission configuration
  • Employee access and integrations require implementation and maintenance
With Kymo

An enterprise portal over the same engine

  • Unified Teams SSO login
  • Granular RBAC aligned to the org structure
  • Application directory with an approval flow
  • Security audit logs on our own server

Kymo provides the employee-facing portal over Dify. Business users access approved applications; authorised builders use the development workspace.
